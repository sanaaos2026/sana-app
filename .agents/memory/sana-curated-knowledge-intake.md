---
name: Sana curated knowledge intake
description: Governance rule for reconciling uploaded knowledge documents with Google Drive copies.
---

Uploaded knowledge and Google Drive material must enter a private, owner-scoped review inbox before any part can become shared or diagnostic knowledge. Exact Drive duplicates collapse by content hash; semantically related documents remain separate sources with explicit relationships.

**Why:** Drive can contain duplicate copies and neighboring project knowledge with overlapping operating rules. Merging by title or topic would leak project facts or silently replace conflicts.

**How to apply:** Compare hashes first, record source links and unresolved differences, keep rights pending until reviewed, and promote only reusable operating logic into the existing canonical framework through an explicit versioned release.
