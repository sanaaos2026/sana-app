---
name: Sana v1 launch model
description: Launch boundary and gating decision for Sana Scan v1.0.
---

Launch Sana Scan v1.0 first as a limited, paid, human-reviewed diagnostic service. The approved commercial offer is 4,500 SAR per case with a 3–5 business-day target.

Do not describe v1 as a fully automated, public self-service SaaS until the unified Scan pipeline, evidence taxonomy, executive report, 90-day plan, tenant-isolation tests, and production hosting are all proven.

**Why:** The app already has useful intake, evidence, decision, and task foundations, but the sellable end-to-end Scan output and independent production environment are not yet complete. A concierge pilot can validate value without overstating automation.

**How to apply:** Keep early customer volume limited, require human review of diagnosis and reports, measure delivery effort and willingness to pay, and treat the launch gates as release blockers for public expansion.
