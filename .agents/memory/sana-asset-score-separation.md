---
name: Sana asset score separation
description: Durable boundaries between asset strength, evidence confidence, information completeness, and execution completion.
---

Keep Asset Score, Evidence Confidence, and Information Completeness separate. A self-reported or adaptive answer may improve completeness and only modestly improve confidence; it must not change Asset Score. Completing a task records execution, not impact.

**Why:** Treating an answer or a checked-off task as business improvement manufactures progress. Asset strength may change only through a small, explained adjustment tied to verified evidence or a documented Before/After impact result.

**How to apply:** Automatically save adaptive answers and refresh the same case Scan, but preserve the asset score. Include source IDs and reasons for any bounded score change, and never translate Sana Score into money.
