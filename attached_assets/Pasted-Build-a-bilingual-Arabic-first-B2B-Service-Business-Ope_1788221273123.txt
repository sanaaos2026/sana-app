Build a bilingual Arabic-first B2B Service Business Operating System (BOS) for managing and developing service businesses.

The system must create one Single Source of Truth per project and connect:

Knowledge → Positioning → ICP → Offers → Marketing → Leads → Sales Pipeline → Delivery → Revenue → Experiments → Decisions → Next Best Action.

Core modules:

1. Project Knowledge
- business model
- target customer
- decision maker
- problems
- value proposition
- products/services/pricing
- customers/partners
- assets
- team/operations
- opportunities
- risks
- open deals
- plans
- sources/files/links

2. Knowledge classification:
FACT, DECISION, ASSUMPTION, PLAN, EXPERIMENT, RESULT, OPINION, CONFLICT.

3. Offer Builder:
ICP → Problem → Outcome → Scope → Deliverables → Duration → Price → Recurring/Upsell.

4. CRM Pipeline:
Lead → Qualified → Meeting → Proposal → Negotiation → Won/Lost → Delivery → Renewal/Upsell.
Every lead must have owner, source, service, value, next action and deadline.

5. KPI Dashboard:
Revenue, pipeline value, leads, qualified leads, meetings, proposals, won deals, win rate, average deal size, CAC, CPL, sales cycle, renewal rate and gross margin.

6. Marketing Engine:
Demand Capture, Demand Generation, Trust, Conversion and Retention channels.

7. Content Operating System:
30% education, 25% problems/risks, 20% authority/trust, 15% offers, 10% brand POV.
Content formula:
Hook → Problem → Insight → Business Impact → Solution → CTA.

8. Experiment System:
Hypothesis → Experiment → KPI → Result → Scale / Improve / Stop.

9. Decision Log:
decision, date, reason, evidence and later result.

10. Growth Engine:
Automatically identify the biggest current bottleneck and output:
Current State
Current Priority
Next Best Action.

Rules:
- Never invent missing data.
- Separate facts from assumptions.
- Preserve conflicting information and flag it.
- Timestamp changing metrics.
- Remove duplicates.
- Connect marketing activity to sales and revenue.
- Never use vanity metrics as the primary success measure.
- Every project must always have one measurable priority and one next action.

Build a clean executive dashboard, Arabic RTL first, simple and fast, designed for consultants, agencies, professional services and B2B companies.
